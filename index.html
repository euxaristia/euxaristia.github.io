How I build

Speed is a feature.

Great software should never make the hardware feel slow. Performance comes first, from the first interaction to the last render.

Listen, then build.

User feedback is not an afterthought. It decides what belongs in a product, what needs refinement, and what should stay out.

Work in good faith.

Politics and opinions stay out of the critical path. Clear thinking, generous collaboration, and good intentions move projects forward.
